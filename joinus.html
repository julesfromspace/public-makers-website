Become a Public Maker!

  • 01Application

    • Apply in March or October
    • Stage 1: Written Application
    • Stage 2: Interview

  • 02Onboarding & Department Selection

    • Meet your Onboarding-Buddy
    • Complete the tasks on the checklist
    • Choose an area you want to contribute to:
    • Board
    • Brand & Communications
    • Partnerships
    • Micro Masterclasses
    • Hackathons

  • 03Active Member

    • Become a part of Public Makers worldwide network
    • Choose the projects you want to work on
    • Contribute to the projects or drive your own!

  • 04Start Alumni

    • Become alumni after two active semesters
    • Give back by mentoring, sharing and supporting new makers
    • Stay involved as much as you want
