Our partners

We bridge the gap between fresh ideas and institutional constraints. Co-create tested solutions with our network and drive real change from the inside out.

We build the people who drive it from the inside.

Most programs ask young talent to arrive with a finished idea. We start earlier. We help the next generation build a clear value compass and connect them directly with the realities of public administration. By partnering with us, you aren't just sponsoring an event. You are investing in a self-sustaining network that shifts the system.

Why partner with us?

  • We provide direct access to young professionals who are oriented, practiced, and connected from day one. Through our collaborative labs and bootcamps, we co-create documented, replicable solutions for your actual administrative challenges.

  • We combine the speed and creativity of the startup world with the depth and accountability of public administration. The outcomes are practical, deployable, and ready for real-world conditions.

  • Your support strengthens the next generation's ability to step into public roles with confidence — building long-term capacity for the institutions that shape our common future.

What sets us apart

Driven by peers. Trusted by institutions.

We hold the patronage of state ministers and partner with federal offices and speak the language of young talent and top-level administration. No other program in Germany holds both at once.

Real constraints, real impact.

Our formats come from the startup world, but the constraints come directly from public administration. Our network leaves with practical experience from real ministerial projects.

Together with our partners we work towards creating meaningful change, fostering innovation, and addressing challenges in the public sector.
